Yes, I drive what I review. The Highland genuinely converted me — I came in sceptical and left impressed. Charging at home overnight means I start every day with a full "tank". The only thing I miss is engine noise, which I've partially solved with a very questionable V8 sound system.

  • 🛣️ ~18,000 km in 11 months
  • 🎨 Pearl White
  • 🔊 Has a ridiculous fake exhaust sound enabled

1998 Mazda MX-5 NA

Not a daily — this is a weekend toy and I'm not apologising for it. 120 hp and absolutely zero driver aids means every roundabout is an event. I bought it for €4,500 and have spent about €3,000 keeping it perfect. Worth every cent.

  • 🔧 Fully restored, new suspension, Torsen LSD
  • 🛣️ 178,000 km (original engine)
  • 🎨 Classic Red — as God intended
  • 🏁 Autocross regular on Sunday mornings

2019 BMW 330i xDrive

I wanted an M3 but my wallet said otherwise, so I got the next best thing. The 330i is more than just a sensible alternative — it's a genuinely great car that still rewards a spirited drive. The xDrive makes it unstoppable in Dutch winters, and the interior still feels premium three years in.

  • 🔧 Stock, H&R springs, 20" Styling 436
  • 🛣️ ~51,000 km
  • 🎨 Alpine White
  • ☕ Has never been to a track, but looks fast at the coffee corner
